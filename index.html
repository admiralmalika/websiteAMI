CORPORATE PROFILE

Who is PT. Admiral Malika Internasional?

PT. Admiral Malika Internasional operates as a trusted trade parent holding company integrating agricultural supply chains and industrial commodities from Indonesian producers directly to international markets.

Integrated Trade Holding

Governing independent business units engaged in commodity sourcing, value-added processing, and transcontinental export logistics.

Farmer & Producer Aggregator

Partnering closely with agricultural networks, local farmers, and processors to ensure continuous supply meeting international quality standards.

Quality Standards & Export Certification

Enforcing ISO 22000, HACCP, Phytosanitary certification, USDA/EU Organic standards, and Incoterms (FOB/CIF) logistics handling.
