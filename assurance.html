INTERNATIONAL TRADE ASSURANCE

Global Supply Chain, Compliance & Export Fulfillment

Every shipment dispatched under PT. Admiral Malika Internasional is governed by rigorous trade protocols, statutory export compliance, and trans-oceanic transit protection.

Incoterms & Loading Ports

Flexible delivery terms under ICC Incoterms® 2020 (FOB, CIF, CFR). Primary ocean dispatches originate from Indonesia's premier deep-water hubs: Tanjung Priok Port (Jakarta, IDJKT) and Tanjung Perak Port (Surabaya, IDSUB).

Statutory Documentation & Compliance

Full documentation guarantee: Official Certificate of Origin (COO / Form A, AK, IJEPA), Agricultural Quarantine Phytosanitary Certificates, Fumigation (AQIS standard), Commercial Invoices, and Ocean Bills of Lading.

Export-Grade Protective Packaging

Engineered packaging preserving cargo integrity: Food-grade vacuum pouches, GrainPro hermetic liners, multi-wall moisture-barrier bags, container desiccant dry-bags, and reinforced OEM master cartons.

Carrier Relations & Transit Tracking

Direct volume partnerships with premier international container carriers (Maersk, MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd, ONE) ensuring guaranteed space allocations, live GPS tracking, and end-to-end voyage reliability.
